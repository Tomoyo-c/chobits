Welcome to my website ✧

Hey, welcome to my corner of the internet.

This is a space where I collect pieces of my life — the things I make, the music I listen to, the places I go, and the moments I don't want to forget.

I like roller skating through quiet streets, drawing whatever comes to mind, and getting lost in music for a while. Sometimes I create things, sometimes I just wander around and let the day happen.

No perfect plans, no particular destination. Just me, a little curiosity, and whatever comes along the way.

This website is my own tiny world on the internet. A place for random thoughts, late-night playlists, personal projects, and little fragments of everyday life.

Feel free to look around, stay for a song, or leave a message in my guestbook.

i'll try to post as consistantly as i can, and my website isn't finished yet but i'll finish it as soon as i can.

Take your time. You're welcome here. ♡
